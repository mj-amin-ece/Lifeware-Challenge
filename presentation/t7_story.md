# t7 The Tax Audit: how we found Neuron's tax rules

Slides for the t7 part of the presentation. Plots in `presentation/plots/`, regenerated by `research/t7_plots.py`.

## 1. The problem: no targets, a 40 + 30 row leaderboard

- Neuron's tax module: Swiss withholding tax, Italian substitute tax and stamp duty, German capital gains tax with
  solidarity surcharge and church tax; 2,493 payouts and 7,259 policy years since 1994.
- No targets at first, two examples, and an hourly leaderboard of 40 payout and 30 annual rows.
- Plot: `t7_rule_timeline.png` (every rate in NRTAXR is a rule change: COBOL -> Neuron NG in 2007, the Italian
  rate steps of 2012 and 2014, the wrong 0.15 % duty corrected by TAXFIX19 in 2019, church tax from 2009).

## 2. Phase 1: weak labels from Neuron's own bookings

- NRTAXB has no amounts, but it says *which* tax Neuron booked for each payout: the gates are exact
  (country 498/498, Swiss threshold on the net payout 1,660/1,660, duty collected 283/283, church tax 88/88).
- The bookings proved the payout is taxed net of the surrender penalty (the only rule that explains every Swiss
  threshold case).

## 3. Phase 2: the leaderboard as an instrument

- One field per upload, then splits: each half of the policies (policy number modulo 4) gets a different rule, so
  one upload tests two to four ideas and the count says which half was right.
- The task's own words became rules: "withheld, accrued **or reported**" + status M "reported to the tax office"
  -> the Swiss tax reported instead of withheld belongs in the annual table (+1 the first time we tried it).
- Plot: `t7_score_progression.png`.

## 4. Phase 3: fitting the organisers' samples, one mismatch at a time

When the organisers published the targets of the first two samples, every mismatching row was traced to its own rule
and each rule was checked on all 110 rows before it was kept:

| Mismatch seen | Rule found | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Swiss untaxed rows with a base | base = amount paid in CHF; elsewhere 0.00; no date without withholding | 33 rows |
| 2017-12-31 vs 2018-01-03 | deadlines move to the next business day, 2 January closed in Switzerland | 6 rows |
| Italian tax above 26 % | each rate on the gain accrued while it was valid (policy value at 31.12.2011, 30.6.2014) | 10 rows |
| German tax 28.71, not 29.36 | KST = base x 25 % / (1 + 25 % x church rate), cents cut off, no partial exemption | 3 rows |
| Italian duty 1 day short | days up to and including the payout day over the days of the year | 7 rows |
| one cent in 2019 rows | the TAXFIX19 correction is one booking, rounded once | 4 rows |
| tax missing in a year | a withheld tax counts in the year it was remitted | 5 rows |

- Plot: `t7_italian_split.png` (a 15 % withdrawal: 847.00 instead of a flat 26 % of 1,355.96, because the gain was
  earned in three rate periods).
- Result: sample 2 payout 40/40, annual 30/30; sample 1 annual 20/20, payout 19/20 (one cent).

## 5. Where the law decides what the samples cannot

- `t7_sample_coverage.png`: the samples checked only some patterns (red cells never). There the rule follows the law
  wherever the samples are silent: Swiss tax 30 days after the end of the month (Art. 16 VStG), dates counted from
  the month the money was paid (PD_PAYDAY, the only field no other task uses).
- `t7_payout_mix.png`: the payout population by year, residence and tax withheld.

## 6. Results and lessons

- Leaderboard: annual 11/30 -> 29/30, payout 1/40 -> 10/40, average 77.01 % -> 88.80 %.
- The leaderboard credits our dated payout rows; the 30 undated rows we match exactly in the published samples are
  not credited there, which we documented instead of overfitting.
- Lessons: weak labels before guesses; one rule per mismatch, checked on every row; law and the data dictionary for
  the cases no sample covers; keep every experiment in a log (research/t7_plan.md).
