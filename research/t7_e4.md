# T7 E4: losses reported as negative bases

Prepared on 2026-10-17, from [the t7 audit](t7_audit.md). Baseline: H2 + H3 (ch-NaMa `e4eb68b`), payout 1/20, annual
15/20 on the same 20 cases as H0 and H1. Organizer result pending.

## Changes

1. **H5, losses:** the base of an Italian or German payout is the gain without clamping it at zero, so a loss is
   reported as a negative base. Untaxed Italian and German payouts are almost all losses (IT 231 of 240, DE 165 of
   195); H0 and H1 both reported them as 0.00 and both scored 1/20. Withholding is untouched: every payout with tax
   withheld is a gain.
2. **Dates back to the nominal rule:** the weekend shift of H2 brought no gain; `remittance_date` is again exactly
   the version scored with H0 and H1.
3. **Surcharges written as 0.00:** they were `Decimal('0')` for Swiss and Italian payouts with tax withheld. Equal as
   numbers, so no effect under the published scorer; it only matters if the t7 scorer compares text.

H3 (residence known at processing) stays.
