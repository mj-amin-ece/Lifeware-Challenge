## Changes

1. **Net of penalty (strong evidence):** a withdrawal or surrender is taxed on the value of the units sold less the
   surrender penalty of the scale by completed policy years (none in waiver periods), rounded by era in the policy
   currency, then converted. This changes the base, tax, surcharges and the Italian stamp duty of 691 payouts and
   100 annual rows.
2. **Death claims (realism, no local evidence):** the amount paid is the death benefit of the option (units sold plus
   the sum at risk), with no penalty; the stamp duty keeps the value of the units. 51 payouts.
3. **No remittance date without withholding (task text):** "remittance date is None if nothing was withheld"; rows
   with only stamp duty collected had an Italian date. 182 payouts; dates now sit exactly on the 498 rows with
   withholding.
